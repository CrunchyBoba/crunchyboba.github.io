What is Floc Modeling?

Flocculation and clarification are critical processes in water treatment that remove suspended particles before filtration. Floc modeling aims to mathematically describe how primary particles aggregate into larger flocs and how these flocs remove contaminants in the clarifier. By understanding these processes, we can:

  • Optimize coagulant dosing to reduce costs and improve water quality
  • Design more efficient clarification systems
  • Enable automated treatment optimization
  • Support the global adoption of AguaClara technology

Mathematical Models

Comparing Clarification Approaches

Coupled Flocculation-Sedimentation Model

Pennock et al. (2023) model linking hydraulic flocculation to clarifier performance.

Primary Particle Concentration:

$$C_P = \left[ \frac{2\pi k_0 e^{\kappa(1-v_c/v_{t,0})} \bar{\alpha} \tilde{G} \theta}{3} \left(\rho_P \frac{\pi}{6}\right)^{-2/3} + C_{P,0}^{-2/3} \right]^{-3/2}$$

where $C_P$ is clarified concentration, $k_0$ is fitted constant, $v_c$ is capture velocity

Performance: RMSE = 0.1867 NTU

✓ Best for effluent turbidity prediction | ✓ Accounts for sedimentation | ✓ Plate settler focused

Discretized Floc Filter Model

Novel grid-based approach enabling spatial analysis of particle removal.

Clarification Equation:

$$C_{p,\text{clarified}} = C_{p,\text{flocculated}} \left[1 - \alpha\pi\left(\frac{3\phi_{\text{floc}}}{4\pi}\right)^{2/3}\right]^{h_{\text{floc filter}}/\Lambda}$$

Accounts for spatial variations and discrete floc interactions

Performance: RMSE = 0.2299 NTU

✓ Better dosage prediction | ✓ Spatial resolution | ✓ Floc sweeping focused

Spring 2025 Continuous Model

Exponential formulation mathematically equivalent to discretized model.

Clarification Equation:

$$C_{\text{clarified}} = C_{\text{flocculated}} \exp\left[-k_c \alpha_c h_{\text{floc filter}}\right]$$

Simpler computational form, ≈3% difference from discretized model

Equivalence: ~99% Match

✓ Faster computation | ✓ Mathematically proven equivalent | ✓ Proven via Taylor series

Spring 2026 Research

Experimental Findings & Analysis

Research Timeline

Dye Tracer Studies
Selected methylene blue for floc visualization. Confirmed adsorption to clay before coagulation.
March 2026
Model Fitting
Fitted both coupled and discretized models to AguaClara plant data from Nicaragua.
April 2026
Analysis & Reporting
Identified coagulant coverage issue and proposed future research directions.

Key Findings

✓ Model Equivalence Proven

Discretized and continuous models are mathematically equivalent through first-order Taylor approximation (2.7% error).

⚠️ Coagulant Coverage Problem

Both models predict unrealistically high coverage (>0.9) at low turbidities, suggesting missing mechanisms.

✓ Model Improvements Successful

Revised mini clarifier design improved reliability and enabled better data collection with dye tracers.

Reference Documentation

Variable Definitions

Complete list of all mathematical variables used in models with descriptions and typical values:

  • $C_P$ - Primary particle concentration (mg/L)
  • $\alpha$ - Attachment efficiency (0 to 1)
  • $\phi_{\text{floc}}$ - Floc volume fraction (≈0.0065)
  • $r_{\text{floc}}$ - Floc radius (1-5 mm)
  • $v_c$ - Capture velocity (0.21 mm/s AguaClara standard)
  • $h$ - Clarifier height (≈710 mm)
